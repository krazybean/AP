# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Don't be a jerk. We're here to build good infrastructure.

## The longer version

AP welcomes contributors of all backgrounds. In return, we ask:

- Be respectful in issues, PRs, and discussions. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks. Ever.
- Assume good faith until there's clear evidence otherwise.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to the maintainer (see package.json author field).
The maintainer may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently remove participants
who violate this code. Decisions are final but not arbitrary — patterns matter
more than single incidents.
